Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Drift - AI Sleep & Meditation ("Drift", "we", "our", or "us") is developed and operated by AppLab Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and an encrypted password. We do not store passwords in plain text.

Usage Data: We collect data about how you use the app, including:

  • Sleep session duration and frequency
  • Sound preferences and favorites
  • Breathing exercise usage
  • Sleep scores and mood ratings
  • Dream journal entries (stored encrypted)
  • Achievement progress

AI Coach Conversations: Messages you send to the AI Sleep Coach ("Luna") are sent to our server, which forwards them to our AI inference provider, OpenRouter, to generate a response. We do not permanently store your conversation history on our servers -- each request only includes your most recent messages, needed to generate that reply. If you use the "Report" option on a Luna response, the reported message is stored so our team can review it. Do not include sensitive personal information (such as financial or medical account details) in your messages to Luna.

Crash and Error Reports: We use Sentry to collect crash reports and error diagnostics so we can fix bugs. These reports are stripped of your email address and IP address before being sent, and are tagged only with an internal user ID.

Device Information: We may collect basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and compatibility purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Drift app experience
  • To sync your data across devices when you sign in
  • To track your sleep progress and streaks
  • To send bedtime reminders (only if you enable notifications)
  • To provide personalized sound recommendations

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud platform with enterprise-grade security. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Session tokens are stored using encrypted device storage (Secure Store).

We implement Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables, ensuring you can only access your own data.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following service providers to operate Drift. We do not sell your data, and these providers only receive what's needed to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, authentication, and file storage
  • OpenRouter: processes messages you send to the Luna AI Sleep Coach in order to generate a response
  • Sentry: receives crash and error reports (with your email and IP address removed) to help us fix bugs

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We do not use your data for advertising purposes. We do not share your data with data brokers or advertising companies.

Guest Mode

You can use Drift without creating an account. In guest mode, all data is stored locally on your device and is not synced to our servers. If you uninstall the app, this data will be lost.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications and use guest mode

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Drift is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Push Notifications

Drift may send push notifications for bedtime reminders and streak encouragement. You can disable these at any time through your device settings or within the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
